### What's new

- **Remember me on this computer.** The sign-in page has a new box. With it ticked you don't have to sign in when you open the app, for 30 days since you last used it. The sign-in is stored encrypted in `%APPDATA%\MojeKocky\session.bin`; signing out deletes it and changing the password ends every sign-in of the account. Without the box nothing changes: the app asks for the password again after the window is closed.
- **Text sizes are right again.** Captions, subtitles and small print (under the amounts on cards, in tables, dialogs and settings) were as large as body text and card titles did not stand out. Small print is smaller again and titles stronger, as designed.
- **The privacy policy** mentions the remembered sign-in, also in the storage table (the `session.bin` file). After the update a notice about the changed policy is shown until you confirm it.
- **The installer is fully in English** when you pick English at the start: the WebView2 question, the licences item in the Start menu, the questions when uninstalling and the messages about an older version. The window shown when the app fails to start or is already running speaks the Windows language.

### Installing and updating

The installer is not code-signed. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**, then allow the changes to your device (the installer asks for administrator rights, on a standard account also for an administrator's password). If Windows says it cannot access the file, your antivirus blocked the program: Windows Security → Protection history → Allow on device.

From an older 0.1.x version the installer removes the old install itself and keeps your data; details in the [1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

**Restoring a backup:** close the app, delete `lego.db-journal`, `lego.db-wal` and `lego.db-shm` in `%APPDATA%\MojeKocky` (if they are there) and copy the backup from `backups\` over `lego.db`. Details in the [README](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop#backup-on-update).

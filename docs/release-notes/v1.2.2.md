### Opravy

- **Figúrky, ktoré Brickset vedie ako sety, sa v Sériách rátajú ako tvoje.** Mighty Machines (42233-1 až -8) má Brickset ako samostatné sety Technic 2026. ### Fixes

- **Figures that Brickset lists as sets now count as yours in Themes.** Brickset lists Mighty Machines (42233-1 to -8) as separate Technic 2026 sets. The theme showed them as "Missing" even though you have them in Minifigures; now they show as "Owned" and count towards the year and the theme. Figures of other series that Brickset does not list in the theme (Shrek, for example) stay out, as do sealed bags.

### Installing and updating

The installer is not code-signed. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**, then allow the changes to your device. The installer asks for administrator rights, and on a standard account also for an administrator's password. If Windows says it cannot access the file, your antivirus blocked the program: Windows Security → Protection history → Allow on device.

From an older 0.1.x version the installer removes the old install itself and keeps your data; details in the [1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

**Restoring a backup:** close Moje kocky, delete `lego.db-journal`, `lego.db-wal` and `lego.db-shm` in `%APPDATA%\MojeKocky` (if they are there) and copy the backup from `backups\` over `lego.db`. Details in the [README](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop#backup-on-update).

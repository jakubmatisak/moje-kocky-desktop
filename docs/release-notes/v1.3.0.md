### Novinky

- **Automatická denná obnova cien.** V Nastaveniach → Dáta na karte BrickEconomy zapneš „Obnovovať ceny automaticky každý deň“, vyberieš čas (predvolene 7:00) a počet cien na jeden beh (predvolene 80). Ceny sa obnovia aj vtedy, keď Moje kocky nie sú otvorené, cez Plánovač úloh Windows: stačí zapnutý počítač a prihlásenie do Windows. Keď bol počítač v ten čas vypnutý, obnova dobehne po zapnutí. Platia tie isté pravidlá ako pri tlačidle (ceny staršie ako týždeň, najprv tie bez ceny, denný limit). Bez kľúča BrickEconomy sa nedá zapnúť. Pod prepínačom aj v tlačidle obnovy v hornej lište vidno, kedy obnova naposledy bežala a koľko cien obnovila. Odinštalovanie úlohu z Plánovača zmaže.
- **Cena mojich figúrok v sérii.** Stránka série vo Figúrkach má kartu ako detail jednej figúrky: kúpené spolu, hodnota spolu, zisk, graf vývoja portfólia série (ako na Prehľade: od prvého nákupu, každá figúrka až odo dňa kúpy) a tlačidlo „Obnoviť ceny mojich figúrok“, ktoré obnoví len figúrky, ktoré máš. Keď má séria duplikáty, karta upozorní, že sa ich cena pripočítava; prepínač „Hodnota jednej série bez duplikátov“ ráta každú figúrku raz a ide pri kompletnej sérii.
- **Viac figúrok naraz.** Na stránke série tlačidlo „Vybrať na úpravu“ zapne výber figúrok, aj chýbajúcich. „Kúpil som vybrané“ pridá vybrané figúrky (chýbajúce aj duplikáty) jednou sumou, „Zmeniť“ hromadne upraví tie, ktoré máš, a „Zmazať“ ich po potvrdení počtu zmaže aj s fotkami. Nerozbalené sáčky to nezmení.

### Opravy

- **Nerozbalený sáčok série sa už necení ako prvá figúrka.** BrickEconomy cenu celej série nemá a pod holým číslom série vráti prvú figúrku. Sáčok je preto bez trhovej ceny, kým mu nezadáš ručnú.

### What's new

- **Automatic daily price refresh.** In Settings → Data, on the BrickEconomy card, switch on "Refresh prices automatically every day", pick a time (7:00 by default) and how many prices per run (80 by default). Prices are refreshed even when Moje kocky is not open, through Windows Task Scheduler: the computer only needs to be on and you signed in to Windows. If the computer was off at that time, the refresh runs once it is on. The same rules apply as for the button (prices older than a week, those without a price first, the daily limit). It cannot be switched on without a BrickEconomy key. Below the switch and on the refresh button in the top bar you can see when it last ran and how many prices it refreshed. Uninstalling removes the task from Task Scheduler.
- **Value of my figures in a series.** A series page in Minifigures has a card like a single figure's page: paid in total, total value, profit, a chart of the series portfolio over time (like the Overview: from the first purchase, each figure from the day you bought it) and a "Refresh prices of my figures" button that refreshes only the figures you have. When the series has duplicates, the card says their price is added; the "Value of one series without duplicates" switch counts each figure once and works for a complete series.
- **Several figures at once.** On a series page, "Select to edit" lets you pick figures, missing ones too. "I bought the chosen" adds the chosen figures (missing ones and duplicates) for one total, "Change" edits the ones you have, and "Delete" deletes them, photos included, after confirming the count. Sealed bags are left alone.

### Fixes

- **A sealed bag of a series is no longer priced as its first figure.** BrickEconomy has no price for a whole series and returns the first figure under the bare series number. The bag stays without a market price until you enter one manually.

### Privacy

The privacy policy's storage table has a new row about the Windows Task Scheduler task; after the update a notice about the changed policy is shown once.

### Installing and updating

The installer is not code-signed. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**, then allow the changes to your device. The installer asks for administrator rights, and on a standard account also for an administrator's password. If Windows says it cannot access the file, your antivirus blocked the program: Windows Security → Protection history → Allow on device.

From an older 0.1.x version the installer removes the old install itself and keeps your data; details in the [1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

**Restoring a backup:** close Moje kocky, delete `lego.db-journal`, `lego.db-wal` and `lego.db-shm` in `%APPDATA%\MojeKocky` (if they are there) and copy the backup from `backups\` over `lego.db`. Details in the [README](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop#backup-on-update).

### Novinky

- **Zisk za obdobie na Prehľade.** Keď v grafe portfólia zvolíš obdobie (1M, 6M, 1R, tento rok alebo vlastné od–do), pod dlaždicami sa ukáže, o koľko sa za ten čas zmenil nerealizovaný zisk v eurách aj v percentách, aký bol na začiatku a aký je teraz. Peniaze za sety kúpené v tom období sa za zisk nerátajú a predaje sú uvedené zvlášť.
- **Nákupy a predaje priamo v grafe ceny.** V detaile setu aj v karte série je v deň nákupu zvislá čiarkovaná čiara a pri osi značka; po prejdení myšou povie, koľko kusov a za koľko si v ten deň kúpil (pri predaji predal).
- **Graf ceny série ako pri jednej figúrke.** Karta „Cena mojich figúrok“ ukazuje súčet histórie cien tvojich figúrok z BrickEconomy, nie vývoj od dátumu nákupu. Keď niektorá figúrka staršiu cenu nemá, ráta sa jej prvá známa cena a pod grafom je napísané, do kedy je súčet odhad.
- **Obdobie aj v grafe ceny.** Graf v detaile setu a v karte série má rovnaký výber obdobia ako Prehľad: 1M až Všetko a polia od–do. Krivka sa oreže na obdobie a nákupy mimo neho zmiznú.

### Opravy

### What's new

- **Profit for a period on the Overview.** When you pick a period in the portfolio chart (1M, 6M, 1Y, this year or your own from–to), a line below the tiles shows how much the unrealised profit changed in that time, in euros and per cent, what it was at the start and what it is now. Money spent on sets bought in that period does not count as profit, and sales are shown separately.
- **Purchases and sales right in the price chart.** In a set's detail and on the series card, a dashed vertical line marks each purchase day with a marker at the axis; hovering it tells you how many pieces you bought that day and for how much (or sold).
- **Series price chart like a single figure's.** The "Value of my figures" card shows the sum of your figures' BrickEconomy price history, not a curve from the purchase dates. When a figure has no older price, its first known price is used and a note below the chart says until when the sum is an estimate.
- **Period in the price chart too.** The chart in a set's detail and on the series card has the same period picker as the Overview: 1M to All and from–to fields. The curve is clipped to the period and purchases outside it disappear.

### Fixes

- **The series card refreshes right away.** After "I bought the chosen", a bulk edit or deleting figures, the card's totals and chart load again; no need to reload the window.

### Installing and updating

The installer is not code-signed. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**, then allow the changes to your device. The installer asks for administrator rights, and on a standard account also for an administrator's password. If Windows says it cannot access the file, your antivirus blocked the program: Windows Security → Protection history → Allow on device.

From an older 0.1.x version the installer removes the old install itself and keeps your data; details in the [1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

**Restoring a backup:** close Moje kocky, delete `lego.db-journal`, `lego.db-wal` and `lego.db-shm` in `%APPDATA%\MojeKocky` (if they are there) and copy the backup from `backups\` over `lego.db`. Details in the [README](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop#backup-on-update).

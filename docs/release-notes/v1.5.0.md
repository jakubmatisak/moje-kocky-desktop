### Novinky

- **Moje figúrky.** Vo Figúrkach je nový prepínač **Série / Moje figúrky**. Moje figúrky sú tvoje jednotlivé figúrky zo sérií (aj nerozbalené sáčky) na rovnakej obrazovke ako Zbierka: karty aj tabuľka, hľadanie, zoradenie a celý panel filtrov vrátane **Umiestnenia a Krabice**, takže hneď vidíš, kde ktorá figúrka leží a čo je v krabici. - **Hodnota figúrok aj pri sériách.** Zoznam sérií vo Figúrkach má hore ten istý riadok Kúpené · Hodnota · Zisk za všetky tvoje figúrky.

### What's new

- **My minifigures.** Minifigures has a new **Series / My minifigures** switch. My minifigures are your single series figures (sealed bags too) on the same screen as the Collection: cards or a table, search, sorting and the full filter panel including **Location and Box**, so you can see right away where each figure is and what is in a box. Above them a **Bought · Value · Profit** line follows the filter, and bulk editing moves several figures at once, for example to another box.
- **Figure value on the series list too.** The series list in Minifigures shows the same Bought · Value · Profit line for all your figures.
- **A missing figure opens.** Click anywhere on the card of a figure you are missing from a series to open its detail and check its current price; it is saved with the date. The "I have it" and "Add to wishlist" buttons on the card stay. Missing sets in Themes open the same way.

### Installing and updating

The installer is not code-signed. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**, then allow the changes to your device. The installer asks for administrator rights, and on a standard account also for an administrator's password. If Windows says it cannot access the file, your antivirus blocked the program: Windows Security → Protection history → Allow on device.

From an older 0.1.x version the installer removes the old install itself and keeps your data; details in the [1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

**Restoring a backup:** close Moje kocky, delete `lego.db-journal`, `lego.db-wal` and `lego.db-shm` in `%APPDATA%\MojeKocky` (if they are there) and copy the backup from `backups\` over `lego.db`. Details in the [README](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop#backup-on-update).

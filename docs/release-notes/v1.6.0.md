### Novinky

- **Ceny vo Figúrkach ako v Zbierke.** Zoznam sérií aj stránka série ukazujú pri každej sérii a figúrke **Kúpené → Hodnota**, zisk v % a dátum ceny, na kartách aj v tabuľke, kde sa dá podľa nich aj zoradiť. Séria ráta súčet tvojich figúrok; keď máš niektorú figúrku viackrát, karta pod sumami povie, že súčet ráta aj duplikáty.
- **Cena figúrky, ktorú nemáš.** Chýbajúca figúrka v sérii, ktorú zbieraš, ukáže aktuálnu cenu na trhu s dátumom a štítkom **„Nemám, len cena“**. Obnova cien ju pýta raz za 30 dní, vždy až za tvojimi kusmi a Chcem (pri piatich sériách je to pár volaní za mesiac).
- **Ceny sa hromadne obnovujú raz za 15 dní.** Hromadná aj automatická denná obnova pýta cenu položky, až keď je staršia ako 15 dní (doteraz týždeň), takže denný limit vystačí na viac položiek. Ručné „Obnoviť cenu“ v detaile platí hneď ako doteraz.
- **Série sa otvárajú rýchlejšie.** Zoznam tém a ročníky uložených tém sa z Brickset pýtajú naraz, nie po jednom; prvé otvorenie Sérií po spustení trvá okolo sekundy namiesto niekoľkých.

### What's new

- **Prices in Minifigures like in the Collection.** The series list and a series page show **Bought → Value**, profit in % and the price date for every series and figure, on cards and in the table, where you can also sort by them. A series sums your figures; if you have a figure more than once, the card says below the sums that duplicates are included.
- **The price of a figure you do not have.** A missing figure of a series you collect shows its current market price with the date and a **"Not mine, price only"** tag. The price refresh asks for it once every 30 days, always after your own items and wishlist (a few calls a month for five series).
- **Prices refresh in bulk once every 15 days.** The bulk and the automatic daily refresh ask for an item's price only once it is older than 15 days (a week until now), so the daily limit covers more items. A manual "Refresh price" in a detail works right away as before.
- **Series open faster.** The theme list and the years of saved themes are asked from Brickset at once, not one by one; the first opening of Series after start takes about a second instead of several.

### Installing and updating

The installer is not code-signed. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**, then allow the changes to your device. The installer asks for administrator rights, and on a standard account also for an administrator's password. If Windows says it cannot access the file, your antivirus blocked the program: Windows Security → Protection history → Allow on device.

From an older 0.1.x version the installer removes the old install itself and keeps your data; details in the [1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

**Restoring a backup:** close Moje kocky, delete `lego.db-journal`, `lego.db-wal` and `lego.db-shm` in `%APPDATA%\MojeKocky` (if they are there) and copy the backup from `backups\` over `lego.db`. Details in the [README](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop#backup-on-update).

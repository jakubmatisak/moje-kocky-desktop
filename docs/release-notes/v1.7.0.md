### Novinky

- **Cena celej série vo Figúrkach.** Na posúdenie ponuky na kúpu ukáže karta série popri tvojich figúrkach aj **Chýba mi** (súčet cien figúrok, ktoré nemáš) a **Celú sériu** (každá figúrka raz, cena nového kusu), zelenou a s počtom figúrok. Karta sa ukáže aj pri sérii, z ktorej nemáš nič.
- **Zelená čiarkovaná čiara Celá séria v grafe** ukazuje, ako sa cena celej série hýbe. Je tam, keď cenu pozná každá figúrka.
- **Zistiť ceny chýbajúcich** stiahne ceny všetkých figúrok série naraz, len na kliknutie. Najprv povie, koľko volaní BrickEconomy to stojí a koľko dnes ostáva, a stiahne až po potvrdení. Figúrky s cenou mladšou než 15 dní sa nepýtajú. Automatická ani hromadná obnova chýbajúce figúrky nepýta ďalej.
- **Zoznam sérií** má stĺpec a riadok Celá séria z cien, ktoré už máš stiahnuté.

### What's new

- **Whole series price in Minifigures.** To judge an offer, the series card shows, next to your figures, **I am missing** (the prices of the figures you don't have) and **Whole series** (each figure once, new price), in green with the number of figures. The card also shows for a series you have nothing from.
- **A green dashed Whole series line in the chart** shows how the price of the whole series moves. It appears when every figure has a price.
- **Get prices of missing figures** downloads the prices of all figures of the series at once, only when you click. It first says how many BrickEconomy calls it costs and how many are left today, and downloads only after you confirm. Figures with a price newer than 15 days are skipped. The automatic and bulk refresh still never ask for missing figures.
- **The series list** has a Whole series column and line, from prices you already downloaded.

### Installing and updating

The installer is not code-signed. When Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**, then allow the changes to your device. The installer asks for administrator rights, and on a standard account also for an administrator's password. If Windows says it cannot access the file, your antivirus blocked the program: Windows Security → Protection history → Allow on device.

From an older 0.1.x version the installer removes the old install itself and keeps your data; details in the [1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

**Restoring a backup:** close Moje kocky, delete `lego.db-journal`, `lego.db-wal` and `lego.db-shm` in `%APPDATA%\MojeKocky` (if they are there) and copy the backup from `backups\` over `lego.db`. Details in the [README](https://github.com/jakubmatisak/moje-kocky-desktop#backup-on-update).
